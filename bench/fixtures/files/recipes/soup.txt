Winter lentil soup (serves 4)

Ingredients
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1 onion, chopped
- 2 carrots, diced
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1/2 teaspoon smoked paprika
- 200 g red lentils
- 1 litre vegetable stock
- 1 tablespoon tomato paste
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1 tablespoon lemon juice

Method
Warm the oil, soften the onion and carrots, then stir in the cumin and paprika.
Add the lentils, stock and tomato paste and simmer for 20 minutes.
Season with the salt, then finish with the lemon juice and a teaspoon of chopped parsley.
